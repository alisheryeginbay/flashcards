Q: What does *AQI* stand for?
A: Air Quality Index

Q: What does *PM* stand for in the context of pollution?
A: Particulate Matter

Q: What's PM2.5?
A: Inhalable fine particles, with diameters that are 2.5 micrometers and smaller.

Q: What's PM10?
A: Inhalable particles, with diameters that are 10 micrometers and smaller.

Q: What are the main types of PM?
A: PM2.5 and PM10
