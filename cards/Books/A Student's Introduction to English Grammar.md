Q: What's syntax study of?
A: The principles governing how words can be assembled into sentences.

Q: What does morphology deal with?
A: The internal form of words.

Q: What does semantics deal with?
A: The principles by which sentences are associated with their literal meanings.

Q: Suppose you want to say that you and your sister were late. How do you do that?
A: *My sister and I were late.*

Q: *I and my sister were late.*
Why is this sentence non-standard?
A: Standard English puts the first-person pronoun last in a coordination.

Q: *It isn’t what you do, it’s the way how you do it.*
What's wrong with this sentence?
A: Standard English uses either *the way* or *how*, not both.

Q: *It isn’t what you do, it’s the way how you do it.*
Fix this sentence.
A: *It isn’t what you do, it’s the way you do it.*
