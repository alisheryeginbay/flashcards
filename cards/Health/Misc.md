Q: What does *NIOSH* stand for?
A: National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health.

Q: Which letters define oil resistance in NIOSH air filtration ratings?
A: N, R, and P

Q: What does N mean in NIOSH air filtration ratings?
A: Not resistant to oil

Q: What does R mean in NIOSH air filtration ratings?
A: Resistant to oil

Q: What does P mean in NIOSH air filtration ratings?
A: Oil-proof

Q: What does the number mean in NIOSH air filtration rating?
A: The minimum percentage of airborne particles the filter removes in testing.

Q: What are the three NIOSH filter efficiency ratings?
A: 95, 99, and 100.

Q: What minimum filtration efficiency does a NIOSH “100” rating require?
A: 99.97%

Q: Which classes define filtering efficiency for European disposable respirators?
A: FFP1, FFP2, and FFP3.

Q: Which European respirator filter efficiency class is roughly equivalent to N95?  
A: FFP2.

Q: What’s China’s respirator rating equivalent to N95?
A: KN95

Q: What’s South Korea’s respirator rating equivalent to N95?
A: KF94
