# Hiragana

## Basic

Q: あ
A: a — A cross with a big loop: someone stabbed an apple and yells "Ah!"

Q: い
A: i — Two little lines side by side, like the two i's in "ski"

Q: う
A: u — A person bent over holding their back: "Ooh!"

Q: え
A: e — A bird on a perch with a hat: "Eh? Where'd my hat go?"

Q: お
A: o — Like あ, but with a ball flying off to the side: "Oh! There goes the ball"

Q: か
A: ka — A blade with a spark flying off: "Ka-chop!"

Q: き
A: ki — Looks like an old-fashioned key

Q: く
A: ku — An open beak going "coo"

Q: け
A: ke — A keg propped next to a post

Q: こ
A: ko — Two short lines, like two coins side by side

Q: さ
A: sa — Like き with a line missing: someone stole a piece of the key, how sad (sa)

Q: し
A: shi — A shepherd's hook: "she" herds sheep with it

Q: す
A: su — A swing with a loop in the rope

Q: せ
A: se — A seat with a tall backrest

Q: そ
A: so — A zigzag thread: sewing (so)

Q: た
A: ta — The letters "t" and "a" squeezed together

Q: ち
A: chi — A person with a big round belly from eating too much cheese

Q: つ
A: tsu — A wave curling over: tsunami

Q: て
A: te — A tentacle hanging down from a line

Q: と
A: to — A toe with a splinter stuck in it

Q: な
A: na — A nun kneeling to pray beside a cross

Q: に
A: ni — A knee (ni) bent next to a wall

Q: ぬ
A: nu — Noodles twisted around chopsticks

Q: ね
A: ne — A cat with a curly tail (neko means cat)

Q: の
A: no — A "no entry" sign with a slash

Q: は
A: ha — An "H" plus an "a": "Ha ha!"

Q: ひ
A: hi — A big grin: "Hee hee!"

Q: ふ
A: fu — A funny face with a tuft of hair on top

Q: へ
A: he — A small hill: "Hey, let's climb it!"

Q: ほ
A: ho — Like は with an extra line, a house with a chimney: "Home!"

Q: ま
A: ma — A ship's mast with two sails

Q: み
A: mi — Looks like "21": me at age 21

Q: む
A: mu — A cow's face with a horn: "Moo!"

Q: め
A: me — A closed eye (me means eye), with no tail

Q: も
A: mo — A fishhook with two worms: "more bait!"

Q: や
A: ya — A yak's head with horns

Q: ゆ
A: yu — A fish making a U-turn: "you" swim back

Q: よ
A: yo — A yo-yo hanging from a finger

Q: ら
A: ra — A rabbit sitting with its ear up

Q: り
A: ri — Two reeds by a river

Q: る
A: ru — Has a loop at the bottom: loop = ru (compare ろ)

Q: れ
A: re — Someone kneeling to re-tie a shoe

Q: ろ
A: ro — Like る without the loop: an open road (ro)

Q: わ
A: wa — Like ね without the tail curl: water splashing out

Q: を
A: wo — A person throwing up their hands: "Whoa!"

Q: ん
A: n — Looks like a handwritten "n"

# Katakana

## Basic

Q: ア
A: a — An axe

Q: イ
A: i — An easel leaning on a stand

Q: ウ
A: u — Like う with a roof on top: "Ooh, shelter!"

Q: エ
A: e — An I-beam in an elevator shaft

Q: オ
A: o — A person with arms flung out: "Oh!"

Q: カ
A: ka — Same as か minus the spark

Q: キ
A: ki — A key with two teeth

Q: ク
A: ku — A cuckoo's beak

Q: ケ
A: ke — A tilted "K"

Q: コ
A: ko — A corner

Q: サ
A: sa — A signpost with a sagging sign

Q: シ
A: shi — A smiling face looking sideways; strokes go up from the side (compare ツ)

Q: ス
A: su — A stick figure sliding down a slide

Q: セ
A: se — Looks like せ

Q: ソ
A: so — One tear dripping down: "so sad"; stroke comes down from the top (compare ン)

Q: タ
A: ta — Like ク with an extra mark: you tagged it

Q: チ
A: chi — A tilted cross with a hat on top

Q: ツ
A: tsu — A tsunami crashing down from above; strokes come from the top (compare シ)

Q: テ
A: te — A telephone pole

Q: ト
A: to — A branch on a totem pole

Q: ナ
A: na — A nail driven in crooked

Q: ニ
A: ni — Two lines, and ni means two

Q: ヌ
A: nu — Chopsticks with a noodle crossing them

Q: ネ
A: ne — A nettle plant with leaves

Q: ノ
A: no — A single slash: "No!"

Q: ハ
A: ha — Two legs walking away laughing: "Ha!"

Q: ヒ
A: hi — The heel of a shoe

Q: フ
A: fu — A fool's hook-shaped hat

Q: ヘ
A: he — Same as へ: "Hey, a hill!"

Q: ホ
A: ho — A holy cross with little legs

Q: マ
A: ma — Mama's pointy hat

Q: ミ
A: mi — Three lines, like three of me

Q: ム
A: mu — A cow's head: "Moo!"

Q: メ
A: me — An X marks a mess

Q: モ
A: mo — Like も without the hook: more lines

Q: ヤ
A: ya — A yak's horn

Q: ユ
A: yu — A U-turn shape: "you" turn around

Q: ヨ
A: yo — A backwards "E" shouting "Yo!"

Q: ラ
A: ra — A rabbit's ear over its head

Q: リ
A: ri — Looks like り: reeds by the river

Q: ル
A: ru — Two roots growing down

Q: レ
A: re — A check mark: "right!"

Q: ロ
A: ro — A square road block

Q: ワ
A: wa — A water tap

Q: ヲ
A: wo — Like ワ with an extra line: "Whoa, that's extra"

Q: ン
A: n — A tilted smile; stroke goes up from the bottom (compare ソ)
