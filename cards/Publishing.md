Q: What is an academic journal?
A: A periodical publication, appearing in regular issues, that publishes peer-reviewed articles reporting original research, reviews, or analysis within a particular academic field.

Q: What is an academic conference?
A: A scheduled event, usually yearly, where researchers gather to present work.

Q: In academia, what are the conference proceedings?
A: A collection of academic papers published in the context of an academic conference or workshop.

Q: What is an academic workshop?
A: Smaller, more focused event, often held for a day or two alongside a big conference.

Q: What is a preprint?
A: A version of scholarly or scientific paper that precedes formal peer review.
