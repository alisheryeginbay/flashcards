Q: What's *k*-fold?
A: *k*-fold is a cross-validation technique where the original sample is partitioned into *k* equal sized subsamples (folds). Of the *k* folds one is retained as the validation data and other *k* - 1 folds are used as training data. This process is then repeated *k* times.
