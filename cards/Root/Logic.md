Q: In propositional logic, what is *modus ponens*?
A: $P \implies Q$. $P$ is true. Therefore, $Q$ must also be true.
#logic

Q: In propositional logic, what is *modus tollens*?
A: $P \implies Q$. Not $Q$. Therefore, not $P$.
#logic

Q: In logic, what is *reductio ad absurdum* also known as?
A: Proof by contradiction
