Q: In PyTorch, what's the method to get the indices of the maximum value of all elements in the tensor?
A: `torch.argmax`

Q: What's the signature of `torch.argmax`?
A: `argmax(input: Tensor, dim: int | None = None, keepdim: bool = False) -> Tensor`

Q: In PyTorch, what's the method to concatenate the given sequence of tensors in the given dimension?
A: `torch.cat`

Q: What's the signature of `torch.cat`?
A: `cat(tensors: tuple[Tensor, ...] | list[Tensor], dim: int = 0) -> Tensor`
