Q: You slept terribly last night. Should you sleep to catch up?
A: No. Get up at the usual time. The lost sleep builds pressure that helps you sleep tonight.

Q: You are lying in bed, unable to fall asleep. What do you do?
A: Get up and come back only when sleepy.

Q: Why it's important to get up when you are unable to fall asleep?
A: Lying awake teaches your brain that bed is a place for being awake. Leaving keeps the bed linked to sleep.

Q: Why it's important to keep a fixed wake-up time every day?
A: It anchors your body clock.
