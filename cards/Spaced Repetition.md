Q: In FSRS, what are the variables that affect memory?
A: Difficulty, stability, and retrievability.
#fsrs #spaced-repetition

Q: In FSRS, what is retrievability?
A: The probability that a person can successfully recall a piece of information at a given moment.
#fsrs #spaced-repetition

Q: In FSRS, what is stability?
A: The time, in days, required for retrievability to drop from 100% to 90%.
#fsrs #spaced-repetition

Q: In FSRS, what is difficulty?
A: The complexity of a particular information, that is, how difficult it is to increase stability after a review.
