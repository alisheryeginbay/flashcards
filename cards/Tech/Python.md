Q: In PyTorch, what's the method to get the indices of the maximum value of all elements in the tensor?
A: `torch.argmax`

Q: What's the signature of `torch.argmax`?
A: `argmax(input: Tensor, dim: int | None = None, keepdim: bool = False) -> Tensor`

Q: In PyTorch, what's the method to concatenate the given sequence of tensors in the given dimension?
A: `torch.cat`

Q: What's the signature of `torch.cat`?
A: `cat(tensors: tuple[Tensor, ...] | list[Tensor], dim: int = 0) -> Tensor`

Q: In `matplotlib`, how to plot a grouped bar?
A: [=`ax.grouped_bar`]

Q: In `matplotlib`'s `ax.grouped_bar`, which parameter is passed to specify categories?
A: [=`tick_labels`]

Q: How to change the tensor `x`'s norm to the tensor `y`'s norm?
A: [=`x = x / x.norm() * y.norm()`]

Q: In `matplotlib`, how to add a horizontal line?
A: [=`ax.axhline`]

Q: In `matplotlib`, what parameter is used to specify the line style?
A: `ls`

Q: In `matplotlib`, what's the equivalent of `dashed` for the line style?
A: [=`--`]

Q: In `matplotlib`, what's the equivalent of `dotted` for the line style?
A: [=`:`]

Q: In `matplotlib`, what's the equivalent of `dashdot` for the line style?
A: [=`-.`]
