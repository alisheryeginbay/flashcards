Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for creating a new window?
A: `prefix c`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for detaching?
A: `prefix d`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for renaming the session?
A: `prefix $`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for side-by-side splitting?
A: `prefix |`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for stacked splitting?
A: `prefix -`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for killing the current window and everything in it?
A: `prefix &`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for killing the current pane?
A: `prefix x`

Q: In tmux, what's the shortcut for switching sessions?
A: `prefix s`
